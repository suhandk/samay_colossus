?? Building better harvest-selling decisions with SAMAY at COLOSSUS 3.0!

Our team, Resonance, participated in COLOSSUS 3.0, where we built SAMAY, an agricultural decision-support platform focused on a practical question:

How much should a farmer sell today, and is holding the remaining harvest financially worthwhile?

That decision involves more than checking a mandi price. Farmers may have immediate cash needs, limited storage, transport expenses, and a risk of deterioration while waiting.

SAMAY brings these factors into one workflow:

?? Cash-first planning ? Prioritize immediate cash requirements when evaluating selling strategies.

?? Storage and expense assessment ? Consider storage capacity, holding duration, deterioration, transport, labour, and transaction costs.

?? Strategy comparison ? Evaluate immediate sale, partial sale, and holding when the required evidence and assumptions are available.

?? Transparent market information ? Show dated observations and sources, distinguish buyer quotations from mandi reference prices, and disclose forecasting limitations.

??? English and Kannada support ? Provide a guided mobile interface and an installable web app.

Our guiding principle:

Cash requirement first. Financial feasibility second. Future selling opportunity third.

SAMAY recommends. The farmer decides.

Built with React, TypeScript, FastAPI, Python, SQLAlchemy, and scikit-learn.

We didn?t win, but we?re proud of what we built and the lessons we?re taking forward. The experience taught us how much reliable data, sound financial calculations, and clear explanations matter when building decision-support tools.
