# Engineering contract

Cash requirement first. Financial feasibility second. Future selling opportunity third.

SAMAY recommends. The farmer decides.

- No fake market prices, fabricated forecasts, fabricated model performance or fabricated financial improvements.
- No production demo mode or judge mode.
- No hardcoded sample recommendations or synthetic production records.
- No frontend-only financial calculations or forced hold recommendations.
- No guaranteed-profit claims or silent fallback to stale information.
- No fabricated API responses or pretending unavailable features are implemented.
- Internal test fixtures must never enter production datasets or farmer-facing screens.
- Distinguish observed data, forecasts and farmer-entered assumptions.
- Store crop and mandi information in configuration or the database. Validate combinations against actual observations.
- Allow anonymous planning and optional account history with minimal personal data.
